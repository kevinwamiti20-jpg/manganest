# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Manga enthusiasts and regular readers who want one place to discover series, track reading progress, save collections, and engage with community picks and reviews.

## Product Purpose

MangaNest helps readers quickly find worthwhile manga, continue ongoing reads, and participate in social discovery without friction. Success means users can move from browsing to reading decisions in minutes, with clear navigation across Home, Explore, Community, and Collections.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, modern, community-driven.

The product should feel curated and immersive, while still fast and task-focused for repeat daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid glassmorphism-heavy cards and over-decorated frosted UI patterns.

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics that flatten personality or make manga discovery feel corporate.

## Design Principles

1. Discover first: users should understand where to explore next within one glance.
2. Reading flow over decoration: interactions should prioritize speed, clarity, and continuity.
3. Cinematic, but disciplined: visual identity is expressive, component behavior stays predictable.
4. Community as signal: social proof should help decisions, not distract from core reading tasks.
5. Navigation confidence: movement between sections should feel intentional, smooth, and orientation-preserving.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve strong text contrast, and ensure keyboard/focus visibility across navigation, drawers, and interactive controls.
